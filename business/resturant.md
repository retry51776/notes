# Restaurant

1. First Question: Can This Restaurant Stay Alive?

- What is the daily revenue?
- What is the gross margin? range 50-70%
- How much is the rent?
- Labor cost?
- Utilities and miscellaneous costs?
- What is the break-even point?

2. Evaluate the Owner’s Risk Tolerance

- Total investment amount?
- Any loans?
- Mortgaged house?
- Franchise or self-operated?
- First time in restaurant business?
- Previous work experience?

3. Location / Trade Area Analysis

- What stores are nearby?
- Is the foot traffic the correct customer demographic?
- How are competitors doing?
- How much are nearby stores selling?
- Is this actually a restaurant-oriented area?

4. Product Selection Logic

- High-frequency demand
- Standardization
- Repeat purchase rate
- Fast serving speed
- Stable ingredient cost
- Ease of employee training
